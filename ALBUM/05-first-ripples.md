## Concetto

Torniamo al **1983**, appena aperto il canale. Radioamatori e scienziati captano echi deboli — non ancora canzoni, non ancora SOS: solo **increspature**. Qualcosa risponde, confuso. Non sanno ancora da dove.

## Suno — Style

This track opens in a moody '80s synth-pop atmosphere — chorus guitars, analog pads, soft drum machine pattern, and a pulsing synth bass under arpeggiated sequences with chorus and delay. An intimate male tenor vocal enters dry and close on the first verse, floating over minimal percussion and wide reverb. Verse melodies rise and fall with expressive phrasing — not recited on one pitch — with clean chorus-guitar fills answering between vocal phrases. The pre-chorus builds tension and pitch before each chorus lift. The chorus expands into layered harmonies and bright sawtooth synth leads, with gated reverb on snare — cinematic, spacious, but restrained not anthemic. Guitars stay subtle in verses; choruses add clean edge-of-mix texture. The second verse adds octave vocal doubles, synth counter-melody, and shortwave static for contrast. The bridge strips to synth pulse, vocal, and static before the final chorus swells. The outro fades on radio hum and arpeggiators dissolving into silence.
