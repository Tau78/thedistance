**Epoca:** Atemporale *(1983.14159?)*  
**Atto:** IV  
**Ruolo narrativo:** Attraversamento del bore — POV narratore e **she** in navicella.

> *Stile: synth-pop / new wave '80s — atmosfera, poco rock*

## Concetto

La navicella entra nel bore. **Narratore** (I) e **she** attraversano il buco nero. Dolore personale, cicatrici non dette, ritornello *we tuned their songs / send us through the bore* come mantra del viaggio.

Anno simbolico: **1983.14159** (transito × π).

## Suno — Style

This track opens in a moody '80s synth-pop atmosphere — chorus guitars, analog pads, soft drum machine pattern, and a pulsing synth bass under arpeggiated sequences with chorus and delay. An intimate male tenor vocal enters dry and close on the first verse, floating over minimal percussion and wide reverb. Verse melodies rise and fall with expressive phrasing — not recited on one pitch — with clean chorus-guitar fills answering between vocal phrases. The pre-chorus builds tension and pitch before each chorus lift. The chorus expands into layered harmonies and bright sawtooth synth leads, with gated reverb on snare — cinematic, spacious. Guitars stay subtle in verses but drive the arrangement in choruses: clean, edge-of-mix in verses; 80s lead guitar in final chorus. The second verse adds a sequenced bass line, octave vocal doubles, synth counter-melody, and faint radio static for contrast. The spoken-word break collapses to synth hum, voice, and silence. The final chorus blooms at full '80s scale — stacked vocals, lush pads, 80s lead guitar, arpeggiators, nostalgic yet futuristic — before a long outro of synth layers fading into spatial reverb until nothing remains.

synth-pop, new wave, 80s
