## Style — template completo

```
This track opens in a moody '80s synth-pop atmosphere — chorus guitars, analog pads, soft drum machine pattern, and a pulsing synth bass under arpeggiated sequences with chorus and delay. An intimate male tenor vocal enters dry and close on the first verse, floating over minimal percussion and wide reverb. Verse melodies rise and fall with expressive phrasing — not recited on one pitch — with clean chorus-guitar fills answering between vocal phrases. The pre-chorus builds tension and pitch before each chorus lift. The chorus expands into layered harmonies and bright sawtooth synth leads, with gated reverb on snare — cinematic, spacious. Guitars stay subtle in verses but drive the arrangement in choruses: clean, edge-of-mix in verses; 80s lead guitar in final chorus. The second verse adds a sequenced bass line, octave vocal doubles, synth counter-melody, and faint radio static for contrast. [BRIDGE/SPOKEN: adattare per traccia.] The final chorus blooms at full '80s scale — stacked vocals, lush pads, 80s lead guitar, arpeggiators, nostalgic yet futuristic — before a long outro of synth layers fading into spatial reverb until nothing remains.

synth-pop, new wave, 80s
```

## Anti-monotonia strofe

| Problema | Fix nel **Style** | Fix nelle **Lyrics** |
|---|---|---|
| Voce piatta | *expressive phrasing, not recited on one pitch* | Strofe con righe lunghe + corte alternate |
| Strofa uguale al coro | *pre-chorus builds tension* | **[Pre-Chorus]** obbligatorio prima del coro |
| V2 = V1 | *octave doubles, counter-melody, static* | Cambiare sillabe/rime in strofa 2 |
| Mancanza respiro | *guitar fills between phrases* | Righe spezzate su 2–4 sillabe (già in Voyage) |
| Suno ignora | Ripetere in Style: *melodic verse, not spoken* | Evitare strofe tutte same length |
